Frequently asked questions

What is Preplit AI?

A student companion. You set it up once; then Juno keeps your semester: it sits in your lectures, files the notes, texts you before deadlines, gives you a short game every day from your own courses and turns what you learn into a career plan.

Who is Juno?

Your companion inside Preplit AI. Juno remembers your courses, timetable and goals, reaches out when something needs you and answers from your own material. When there is no source, Juno says so.

Do I need to connect my school's LMS?

No. You add your courses, timetable, slides and key dates once, and Juno takes it from there.

Am I allowed to record my lectures?

Ask your professor before you record. Preplit AI is made for recordings you have permission for.

What happens to my recordings?

They are deleted from our server as soon as they have been transcribed. Only the transcript, notes and cards remain.

Can anyone else see my notes?

No. Your notes, cards and conversations are not shared and are never used to train AI models.

How much can I record?

During the pilot, up to 40 lectures a month and up to 4 hours in one recording.

When can I use it?

The pilot opens with 20 students on one course. Join the pilot and we'll tell you when your course is next.
