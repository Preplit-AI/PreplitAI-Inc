Operating Systems · Week 9 · 50-minute recording · 8 cards
Deadlock
Processes each wait for a resource another one holds, so none of them can proceed. It only happens when four conditions hold at once.
Key ideas
  • Mutual exclusion: one process uses a resource at a time.
  • Hold and wait: a process holds one resource while it waits for another.
  • No preemption: a held resource cannot be taken away.
  • Circular wait: the waiting forms a circle.
Dealing with it
Prevention: number the resources and request them only in that order, and circular wait disappears.
Avoidance: the banker's algorithm grants a resource only in a safe state. The steps of the lecturer's worked example were unclear.
Detection and recovery: find a cycle in the resource-allocation graph, then end a process to break it.
Exam hint
“The four conditions will be on the midterm.”

What happens to my recording?

It is deleted from our server as soon as it has been transcribed. Only the transcript, notes and cards remain.

Can anyone else see my notes?

No. Your notes and cards are not shared, and they are not used as training data.

Am I allowed to record lectures?

Ask your lecturer before you record. Junbee is made for recordings you have permission for.

Which files can I upload?

Audio from your phone's voice recorder, up to 200 MB. You can also paste the lecture as text.

How many lectures a month?

Up to 40 lectures a month during the pilot.

Are my notes deleted with my account?

Yes. Your notes, cards and transcripts are all deleted with it.
