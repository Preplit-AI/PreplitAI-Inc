# PreplitAI Pricing Structure

## Overview
PreplitAI uses a three-tier pricing structure: a free tier to drive adoption, a low-cost Student Pro tier priced for a Korean student budget, and a custom-priced Institution tier for universities and academies. The structure is intentionally simple — three tiers, not more — so students can decide in seconds.

---

## Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Price | What's Included |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Free** | **₩0 / month** | Limited uploads, limited AI chats per day, basic quizzes. Lets students try the product with no commitment. |
| **Student Pro** | **₩9,900 – ₩12,900 / month**<br>*(Discounted semester bundle available)* | Unlimited AI chats & uploads, Interactive Learning Mode, video lectures, podcast mode, exam prediction, full analytics. |
| **Institution** | **Custom**<br>*(Contact sales)* | For universities and academies: bulk seats, admin dashboard, integration support, and institution-wide analytics. |

---

## Why This Structure
- **Priced for students, not just priced low**: Student Pro sits well below typical international competitor pricing, positioned as an easy monthly decision for a student budget rather than a premium subscription.
- **Semester billing**: Mirrors how Korean students already budget — tuition and campus services are paid per semester, making a semester bundle a familiar purchase pattern.
- **Three tiers total**: Free to try, Student Pro to commit, Institution for bulk/B2B.
- **Custom Institution pricing**: Scoped per university or academy based on seat count and integration needs, opening a B2B growth path.

---

## Competitive Pricing Note
Student Pro is positioned to undercut comparable international AI study tools while remaining sustainable. The core differentiator is that PreplitAI is localized for the Korean academic environment (Korean Academic Assistant, bilingual interface), offering features unavailable elsewhere at any price.
