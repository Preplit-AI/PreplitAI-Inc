# PreplitAI Master Startup Proposal (Version 2.0)
**Track**: K-Startup Grand Challenge 2026, International Student Track  
**Tagline**: Transform Any Study Material Into an Interactive Learning Experience — Built for Korea.

---

## Executive Summary
PreplitAI is an AI-powered interactive learning platform built for Korean university students and international students studying in Korea. Rather than acting as a generic AI chatbot, PreplitAI transforms static study materials — lecture slides, PDFs, textbooks, handwritten notes, YouTube videos, and recorded lectures — into a personalized, interactive, and fully bilingual (Korean/English) learning experience.

Our starting wedge is localization: PreplitAI is built from the ground up around how Korean universities actually teach, and around the specific barriers international students face navigating Korean-language academic material.

---

## The Problem
Learning is scattered across PDFs, lecture slides, YouTube, generic chatbots, note-taking apps, flashcards, and quiz apps — students spend more time organizing information than learning it.

For students in Korea, the problem is sharper:
- Korean lecture materials and technical terminology are difficult for international students to parse.
- Professors often teach differently from the textbook, with limited support for students still building Korean fluency.
- Existing AI study tools are generic, English-first, and not built for the structure of Korean university courses.

---

## The Solution
PreplitAI turns any learning material into a complete, bilingual AI-powered learning environment tailored to how Korean universities teach. Every uploaded document becomes an interactive classroom, available in both Korean and English.

---

## 9 Core Feature Pillars

1. **Korean Academic Assistant (Our Localization Edge)**
   - Korean ↔ English academic translation
   - Technical terminology explanations in context
   - Course material simplification aligned with how Korean professors structure lectures
   - Full support for Korean-language lecture notes and slides

2. **Interactive Learning Mode (Flagship Feature)**
   - Drag-and-drop exercises
   - Interactive diagrams and visual animations
   - Simulations and concept exploration
   - Step-by-step problem solving
   - Mini educational games and live demonstrations

3. **AI Study Chat**
   - Upload PDFs, lecture slides, books, YouTube videos, recorded lectures, or images
   - Unlimited Q&A with course-wide context retention

4. **AI Video Lecture Generator**
   - Converts notes into AI-generated lecture videos with voice narration and animated diagrams

5. **Podcast Learning Mode**
   - Converts study material into an AI podcast for passive learning while commuting or exercising

6. **Flashcards**
   - Automatically generates personalized active-recall memory cards

7. **Smart Quiz Generator**
   - Unlimited adaptive quizzes (MCQ, short answer, true/false, fill-in-the-blank)

8. **Expected Exam Questions**
   - Analyzes past exams & midterms to predict recurring topics, professor trends, and likely exam questions

9. **AI Learning Analytics**
   - Identifies misunderstood concepts and repeated mistakes to build a personalized study roadmap
