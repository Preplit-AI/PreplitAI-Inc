Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Preplit AI plans and billing.

Why is Preplit AI priced so affordably for students?

We built Preplit AI for university students on a student budget. Student Pro costs less a month than a single textbook.

How does the Semester Pass work?

You pay once for a 4-month term at 25% off, so you're covered from midterms to finals without monthly renewals.

What can I do with the Free plan?

Juno checks in every day, the Lecture Buddy files 5 lectures a month into your course workspaces, and you get the daily mini-games. No credit card needed.

Can I upgrade, downgrade or cancel anytime?

Yes. You can change or cancel your plan at any time, with no hidden fees.

What about the pilot?

During the pilot, every account can record up to 40 lectures a month and up to 4 hours in one recording.
